# VanityBody public portability checkpoint

## Exact public claims

- This workstream publicly records a narrow five-pair correction boundary:
  Wedding Body Suit (Vanilla and SBBF), Satin Thong (Vanilla), Serious Business
  Lady (Vanilla), and Sexy Catsuit (Vanilla). The corresponding root-template
  UUIDs and target visual-resource UUIDs are compatibility metadata.
- The listed BCB modes are protected; the listed SBBF modes for Satin Thong,
  Serious Business Lady, and Sexy Catsuit are protected.
- The public clearance and patch modules operate only on supplied in-memory
  arrays. Portable tests use declared synthetic NumPy fixtures.
- Local route, anchor, GLB, ledger, and audit inputs require an explicit,
  ignored `local/config.json`; missing configuration fails closed and
  integration-only tests skip honestly.

## Explicit non-claims

This checkpoint does **not** contain or claim a corrected GR2, a corrected
mesh, a TEST PAK, a visual-bank override, a complete VanityBody/SCO/Sindae
coverage ledger, collision-free clearance, protected-payload acceptance, route
validity, live BG3/BG3MM activity, or gameplay acceptance. Any local diagnostic
result remains local evidence, not public proof of those outcomes.

## Boundary

No public module discovers or reads ignored `evidence/`, `staging/`, generated
ledgers, or external extracted assets at import time. Generated integration
output is restricted to `local/generated/`; copied/extracted third-party asset
content and private workstation paths must remain outside public files.
